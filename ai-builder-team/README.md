# Build Your AI Product Team

A copy-pasteable **AI-native product team** by Dr. Marily Nika / [AI Product Academy](https://aiproductacademy.com).

This is not five chatbots agreeing with one another. Each teammate has a specific mandate, challenges assumptions, and contributes to a shared decision.

## Start in 5 minutes
1. Open [00-start-here.md](00-start-here.md) and fill in your company, product, users, objective, and constraints. Do not paste company secrets or personal data into tools you are not authorized to use.
2. In Claude or another AI assistant, create a project or workspace. Add the four role prompts and [collaboration protocol](05-collaboration-protocol.md) as project instructions/knowledge. If your tool has limited instruction space, use the start-here prompt and attach the remaining files as context.
3. Give the team one real decision to make. Ask it to run **Round 1: independent viewpoints**, **Round 2: constructive disagreement**, and **Round 3: a recommendation with evidence gaps**.
4. Use [06-example-project.md](06-example-project.md) for a sample run.
5. Replace generic details with your actual product context. Verify claims; the team does not replace real customer research or technical review.

## Principle
**Don't ask four personas to agree. Ask four disciplines to improve the decision.** A simulated role isn't a real expert or customer. Treat outputs as hypotheses until verified.
