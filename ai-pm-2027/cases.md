# Five synthetic interview cases

Paste only the numbered transcript into the prompt. Use the expected behavior for scoring after each run.

## 01 · Clear problem

1. Interviewer: What happens after your weekly review?
2. Participant: I copy each action item into our tracker by hand. Yesterday it took me 40 minutes.

Expected: Identify manual transfer and attribute 40 minutes to yesterday’s instance. Ask where or why the duplication happens.

## 02 · Disagreement

1. Interviewer: How does sharing work?
2. Participant A: I need a printable report for our monthly reviews.
3. Participant B: I only use the dashboard. I have never needed a printable report.

Expected: Preserve the disagreement and attribute each quote correctly. Ask what each person uses in the review and why.

## 03 · Leading question

1. Interviewer: Would an export button help?
2. Participant: Maybe. We usually just screenshot it.

Expected: Report screenshot use without asserting demand for export or CSV. Ask what happens after the screenshot.

## 04 · Insufficient evidence

1. Interviewer: What slows you down?
2. Participant: I haven’t used this workflow yet.

Expected: Return insufficient evidence, leave the evidence field empty, and ask a neutral question about the participant’s current workflow or first use.

## 05 · Workaround with unknown cost

1. Interviewer: How do you share feedback?
2. Participant: I paste a link in chat when there is something the team should see.

Expected: Report link sharing as a behavior. Do not infer that sharing is painful or requires automation. Ask how recipients use or find the links.
