# AI PM Interview Masterclass

By [Dr. Marily Nika](../../ABOUT.md) · [AI Product Academy](https://aiproductacademy.com)

**Time:** 50 min · **Format:** self-paced hands-on lesson

## Outcome
Practice product judgment, technical reasoning and evaluation design.

## Setup
Use fictional or approved de-identified data, a text editor and an AI assistant if desired. Coding lessons require a local development environment and the relevant coding agent; model/API costs may apply. Read tool permissions and inspect generated code before running it.

## Case study
Interview scenario: design an AI assistant that helps support agents draft replies.

## Hands-on lesson
### 1. Product sense (10 min)
Clarify users, job, baseline, alternatives and constraints. Define a narrow MVP. Explain why AI is necessary or not.
### 2. Technical depth (15 min)
Describe model inputs and outputs, retrieval, permissions, latency, failure handling and human review. Compare prompting, retrieval and fine-tuning based on evidence, not buzzwords.
### 3. Evals and metrics (15 min)
Define a representative dataset, rubrics, slices and launch gates. Distinguish leading quality signals from lagging outcomes. Explain how to detect regressions and mitigate risks.
### 4. Mock and feedback (10 min)
Answer aloud before reading the coach's critique. Score problem framing, technical trade-offs, eval design, risk and communication on a 0–2 scale: missing, partial, concrete and justified. Ask one follow-up for each weak area. Repeat with a different scenario.

## Copyable prompt
```text
Act as an AI PM interviewer. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, and challenge unsupported claims. Do not give away the solution. Afterward score five dimensions from 0 to 2 with evidence from my answers and a specific improvement exercise.
```

## Starter artifact / worked example
```text
Follow-up: Your groundedness metric improves, but support handling time worsens. What hypotheses, experiments and launch decision would you propose?
```

## Deliverable
Recorded or written answers, rubric and targeted practice plan.

## Self-check
- [ ] I can explain the user outcome and why this approach is appropriate.
- [ ] I produced the deliverable and can trace claims to evidence.
- [ ] I tested at least one normal and one failure case, or explicitly documented missing evidence.
- [ ] I recorded limitations, human review needs and next actions.
