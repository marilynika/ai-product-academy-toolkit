# About Dr. Marily Nika

I'm an AI product leader, builder, educator, and founder of [AI Product Academy](https://aiproductacademy.com).

I have a Ph.D. in Machine Learning from Imperial College London and spent over a decade building AI products at Google, as well as working at Meta Reality Labs. My work spans conversational AI, speech, intelligent assistants, AR/VR and generative AI.

I'm also an Executive Fellow at Harvard Business School, an O’Reilly author, a LinkedIn Learning instructor, and a TED AI and TEDx speaker.

## Why I built this repository

I believe AI product managers should be builders. Understanding models and writing PRDs is not enough: we need to prototype, design evaluations, understand failure modes, and make evidence-backed product decisions.

I started AI Product Academy in 2022 to make these capabilities accessible to product managers, leaders and aspiring AI builders. This repository brings together practical frameworks, installable agent skills and runnable AI applications as they are released, so that you can learn by doing.

## What I'm building

- Practical AI product management frameworks for humans.
- Reusable skills for AI agents.
- AI applications that help product managers do their jobs better.
- Education and a community for people who want to become AI-enhanced builders.
