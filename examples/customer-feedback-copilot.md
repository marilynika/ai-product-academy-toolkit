# Walkthrough: Customer Feedback Copilot

*Fictional educational example; not a claim about a real product or evaluation results.*

**User problem:** PMs spend hours reading feedback and cannot easily trace reported trends to evidence.

**Goal:** Identify emerging themes while linking each summary to its underlying comments.

**Scope:** Categorize and summarize approved feedback. Do **not** message customers, edit roadmaps or make product decisions automatically.

**What to evaluate:** Theme relevance; factual grounding; coverage of minority feedback; citation traceability; privacy; time saved versus manual workflow. Sample by language, segment and complaint severity.

| Failure mode | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Fabricated customer quote | Misleads planning | Extract exact quotes with source IDs |
| Missing minority concerns | Buries important insights | Evaluate by segment |
| Exposed sensitive content | Privacy breach | Access control and redaction |
| Overstated frequency | Distorts priorities | Show counts and denominators |

**Launch:** Begin with a reviewed internal pilot only after scoped evaluation and privacy checks. Set numerical acceptance criteria using actual domain evidence, not a generic percentage.
