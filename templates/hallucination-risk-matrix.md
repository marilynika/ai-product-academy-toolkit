## Examples
| Use case | Potential failure | Possible mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Naming brainstorm | Irrelevant suggestion | Human editing |
| Customer feedback summary | Fabricated quote | Links to immutable source records |
| Health or finance guidance | Harmful false assertion | Strict boundaries, specialist review, escalation |
| Agent changing account settings | Incorrect real-world action | Scoped authorization, verification, explicit approval and audit |
